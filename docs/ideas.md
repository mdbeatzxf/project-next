# Ideas

Purpose: backlog of product and project ideas. Nothing here is decided unless its status says `accepted`.

## How to add an idea

1. Copy the template entry below.
2. Add your entry at the bottom of the list, with the next number.
3. Open a PR on a branch `docs/idea-<short-name>`.
4. Status starts as `new`. We change it in the weekly sync.

Statuses: `new` → `discussing` → `accepted` or `parked`.
If an idea needs a longer discussion, open a GitHub Issue with the `Idea` template and link it from the entry.

## Template

```markdown
### N. Title
- **Added by:** name
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** new
- **Description:** one or two sentences.
- **Why it matters:** one sentence.
```

## Entries

### 1. Walk-in queue as the hero feature
- **Added by:** kickoff meeting (all)
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** accepted – MVP
- **Description:** Clients join a Provider's walk-in queue remotely, get a position, see how many people are ahead of them and an estimated wait, and get notified when it is nearly their turn. Providers manage the queue from the dashboard ("next", "skip", "done").
- **Why it matters:** This is the one thing no existing platform does for this niche. It is the reason Clients and Providers would switch.

### 3. Survey of 20–50 barbers about their biggest hassle
- **Added by:** kickoff meeting (all)
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** new
- **Description:** Go to barbers and hairdressers and ask what their biggest hassle is. If many say the same thing, start there. Survey questions are a Phase 0 to-do.
- **Why it matters:** Networking over marketing. It validates the problem before we build and gives us the first pilot contacts.

### 4. "Comes to your place" mobile stylists
- **Added by:** kickoff meeting (all)
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** new
- **Description:** Support Providers who work mobile (braiders, home hairdressers) with a service radius instead of an address, and let Clients filter by "comes to your place" vs "you go there".
- **Why it matters:** Many hairdressers in the target group have no shop. Without this they cannot join.

### 5. Optional online payment via PayPal / bank transfer
- **Added by:** kickoff meeting (all)
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** new
- **Description:** Let Clients pay online (PayPal, bank transfer, later in-app) if the Provider enables it. Cash stays the default. Online payment is never required.
- **Why it matters:** Convenience for some Clients and Providers, but forcing it would keep many Providers away.

### 6. Hair extensions / colour ordering add-on (later)

### 10. Verification badge for Providers
- **Added by:** kickoff meeting (all)
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** new
- **Description:** A visible badge on the Provider profile after ID or business verification by the Platform. Trust is built via reviews and portfolio; the badge adds a Platform-checked layer. Basic admin verification is in MVP scope; the badge design and what exactly is checked are open.
